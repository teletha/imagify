imagify.jpeg

JpegImageWriter

  • ImageWriter
  • ImageTranscoder
ImageWriter that encodes images as JPEG using jpegli.

Any non empty RenderedImage can be encoded. Images whose layout the encoder cannot consume directly are converted to non premultiplied java.awt.image.BufferedImage#TYPE_4BYTE_ABGR first, so colour and alpha are preserved rather than composited over an opaque background. The alpha byte is then discarded, because a JPEG cannot store one; an image with transparency is written against whatever colour sits behind it, and a caller that needs the picture has to flatten it first.

The quality comes from the compression quality of the ImageWriteParam, which maps the javax.imageio range of JpegImageWriterSpi#MINIMUM_QUALITY to JpegImageWriterSpi#MAXIMUM_QUALITY onto the encoder's range of 1 to 100. A quality of 0 is raised to 1 rather than refused, which is what the JDK's own JPEG writer does with it, so a caller asking for the smallest possible file is not answered with an exception.

The two settings a JPEG has and an ImageWriteParam has nowhere for are on JpegWriteParam, and default to what imagify.ImageFormat.Jpeg names: 4:2:0 with the standard entropy coder tables.

Instances are stateful and, as mandated by ImageWriter, not thread safe.
