ImageFormat.Jpeg

  • ImageFormat

JPEG format (.jpg, .jpeg). Lossy compression. No alpha support. Magic bytes: no reliable header (starts with 0xFF 0xD8).

The quality says how much of the picture survives, but it says nothing about how the two colour-difference channels are laid down. That is #subsampling: a JPEG may store them at full size, or half the width, or half in both directions, and the choice is orthogonal to the quality. #optimizeHuffmanTables is the other thing the quality does not say, whether the entropy coder tables are the standard ones or computed from the image being written.

Both are carried here rather than being a write argument for the same reason the AVIF and WebP settings are: an javax.imageio.ImageWriteParam has room for a quality and a compression type and for nothing else. They describe the file a caller wants, not one particular write.

DEFAULT_SUBSAMPLINGSubsampling

The colour-difference resolution a JPEG is written at when the format does not say, which is the one libjpeg and every other encoder writes by default.

DEFAULT_OPTIMIZE_HUFFMAN_TABLESboolean

Whether the entropy coder tables are computed from the image when the format does not say, which they are not: the standard tables are used, because a caller has to ask for the extra encode and the extra memory that computing them costs.

subsamplingSubsampling

How finely the two colour-difference channels are stored, which is what decides the sharpness of the edges between areas of colour.

It costs nothing in the quality argument and shows plainly at the same quality: the three are three different files of three different sizes holding three different amounts of the picture. Subsampling#S444 keeps the most and writes the largest, which is what a screenshot of text wants; Subsampling#S420 is the default and the smallest.

optimizeHuffmanTablesboolean

Whether the entropy coder tables are computed from the image rather than taken from the standard set, which makes a smaller file for the same pixels at the cost of a slower encode and a table the decoder has to read rather than one it already knows.
