How a frame is made to fit its cell. The names follow the resizing methods of Imagify.

STRETCHFit

Stretches to the cell exactly, like Imagify#resize(int, int).

INSIDEFit

Keeps the aspect ratio and scales down when the frame does not fit; a frame that already fits is left at its own size. Either way it is centred, so this is what Imagify#padTo(int, int) does per cell.

FILLFit

Keeps the aspect ratio and crops the overflow, like Imagify#resizeToFill(int, int).
