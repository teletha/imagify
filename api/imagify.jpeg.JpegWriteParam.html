JpegWriteParam

  • ImageWriteParam
  • IIOParam

The write parameters of JpegImageWriter, which carry the two settings a JPEG has and an javax.imageio.ImageWriteParam has nowhere to put.

javax.imageio.ImageWriteParam offers a quality and a compression type, and a JPEG's colour-difference resolution and entropy coder tables are neither of those. They are the same two settings imagify.ImageFormat.Jpeg carries, so a caller driving ImageIO directly can ask for exactly what a caller of imagify.ImageWriter gets, rather than the two paths being able to produce different files for the same request.

Every reader of these values has to cast, because getDefaultWriteParam() answers ImageWriteParam. That is the same bargain the JDK's own javax.imageio.plugins.jpeg.JPEGImageWriteParam makes, and the cast is the only way ImageWriteParam can be extended at all: canWriteCompressed, compressionTypes and compressionType are all protected with no setters.
