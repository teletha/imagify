AvifDecoder

  • com.sun.jna.Structure

Decoder state: avifDecoder.

libavif 1.1.0 appended imageSequenceTrackPresent after data and 1.2.0 appended imageContentToDecode after that. Neither member is declared here, so the structure is slightly smaller than the native one; that is harmless because only the leading members - whose offsets are identical in every 1.x release - are ever touched.
