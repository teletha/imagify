AvifImageWriter

  • ImageWriter
  • ImageTranscoder
ImageWriter that encodes images as AVIF using libavif.

Any non empty RenderedImage can be encoded. Images whose layout libavif cannot consume directly are converted to non premultiplied java.awt.image.BufferedImage#TYPE_4BYTE_ABGR first, so colour and alpha are preserved rather than composited over an opaque background.

The encoder speed is fixed at AvifLibrary#DEFAULT_SPEED. The quality comes from the compression quality of the ImageWriteParam, which maps the javax.imageio range of AvifImageWriterSpi#MINIMUM_QUALITY to AvifImageWriterSpi#MAXIMUM_QUALITY onto the libavif range of 0 to AvifLibrary#AVIF_QUALITY_BEST.

Instances are stateful and, as mandated by ImageWriter, not thread safe.
